1. Who We Are

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us or request a quotation, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Property or service address
  • Information about requested cleaning services
  • Messages you send to us
  • Appointment and service records
  • Payment and invoice information, where applicable

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information where necessary to:

  • Respond to customer enquiries
  • Provide cleaning quotations
  • Arrange cleaning appointments
  • Deliver agreed cleaning services
  • Send invoices and manage payments
  • Meet applicable legal obligations
  • Protect our website and business

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Depending on the circumstances, we rely on the following lawful bases under UK GDPR:

  • Contract: Providing agreed services and taking steps requested before entering a contract.
  • Legitimate interests: Responding to general enquiries, protecting the business and managing appropriate business operations.
  • Legal obligations: Keeping records required by tax, accounting or other applicable laws.
  • Consent: Where consent is legally required, such as certain optional cookies or marketing activities.

5. Sharing Your Information

We may share personal information with service providers necessary to operate our business, such as:

  • Website hosting providers
  • Email service providers
  • Contact form providers
  • Booking or invoicing services

  • Authorities, where required by law

6. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including applicable legal requirements.

Where non-essential cookies or similar technologies require consent, we will request that consent before using them.

Simply visiting our website does not constitute consent to optional cookies.

8. Future Google AdSense Advertising

Google AdSense is not currently installed on this website.

If we introduce Google AdSense in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy to explain the advertising technologies, relevant data processing and visitor choices.

  • Access personal information held about you

  • Restrict certain processing activities
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request data portability where applicable

These rights are subject to applicable conditions and legal exceptions.

To exercise your rights, contact: Aaprowindow@hotmail.com
